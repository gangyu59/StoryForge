# StoryForge — Milestones, Rewards & Endings

## 1. Milestone Definition

A milestone is a **system-verifiable state transition**.
It is not a story beat.

Milestones mark irreversible progress.

---

## 2. Milestone Types

1. Relationship
   - NPC trust or dependency crosses a threshold

2. World State
   - Tension / hope reaches a critical band

3. Identity
   - Player behavior stabilizes into a pattern

4. Cognitive
   - Player acquires a complete understanding

---

## 3. Concrete Measurement

Milestones must be machine-checkable.

Example:
- npc.trust >= 0.6
- key_memory exists
- stat pattern detected

No subjective evaluation.

---

## 4. Reward Structure (Three Layers)

1. Narrative Recognition
   - AI explicitly acknowledges progress

2. System Unlock
   - New options, behaviors, or paths become available

3. Persistent Record
   - Journal entry
   - Milestone log

Rewards must change future experience.

---

## 5. Ending Philosophy

StoryForge chapters are **concludable**.

Endings occur when:
- World state stabilizes or collapses
- Key relationships become irreversible
- Player identity is confirmed

Endings are summaries, not judgments.

---

## 6. After an Ending

Players may:
- Restart with a different identity
- Continue to the next chapter
- Stop and reflect

Stopping is a valid outcome.

---

## 7. Design Principle

If a player stops here and feels:
"I walked a complete path"

Then the system succeeded.
